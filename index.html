The Philosophy of the Line

In Persian calligraphy, every stroke carries meaning beyond the word it forms. The reed pen (qalam) is cut at a precise angle. The ink flows from lampblack and gum arabic. The paper is burnished to accept the line. Nothing is incidental.

What You Will Learn

Materials & Tools

How to select and cut a reed pen (qalam), prepare ink, and choose the right paper for practice and final works.

Proportional Systems

The nuqta (dot) system that governs all letter proportions \u2014 the mathematical foundation beneath the beauty.

Three Major Scripts

Nastaliq (the hanging script), Naskh (the foundation), and Shekasteh (the broken style) \u2014 each with its own character.

Historical Context

The lineage from Mir Ali Tabrizi to contemporary masters. Understanding tradition is essential to contributing to it.

Practice Methodology

Structured exercises following the traditional mashq (practice) method used in Persian calligraphy schools for centuries.

Artistic Expression

Moving beyond reproduction to personal expression \u2014 the ultimate goal of any calligraphic practice.
