Classical Forms

Timeless expressions of the Persian calligraphic tradition.

بسم الله الرحمن الرحیم

Bismillah

Nastaliq — The opening phrase of the Quran, rendered in the classical Nastaliq tradition. Every calligrapher begins here.

عشق

Eshq (Love)

Nastaliq — Three letters that contain the full range of calligraphic challenge: the complex eyn, cascading shin, and deep qaf.

ز دست مده یا رب مرا
جز دست آن نگار من

Verse of Hafez

Nastaliq — Poetry and calligraphy are inseparable in the Persian tradition. Hafez's ghazals are among the most commonly rendered texts.

الله

Allah

Naskh — The name of God in the structured, upright Naskh script. Note the contrast with Nastaliq's diagonal flow.

این نیز بگذرد

This Too Shall Pass

Nastaliq — A phrase attributed to Persian Sufi poets. The cascading Nastaliq form mirrors the passage of time.

قلم زبان دل است

The Pen is the Tongue of the Heart

Nastaliq — The calligrapher's creed. The pen does not merely transcribe thought — it expresses the soul.

The Scripts Compared

The same phrase rendered in three different scripts reveals each script's distinct character.

در نام خداوند

Naskh

Upright, structured, measured. Each letter sits clearly on the horizontal baseline. The script of precision.

در نام خداوند

Nastaliq

Flowing, diagonal, cascading. Words hang from the right and descend to the left. The script of poetry.

در نام خداوند

Shekasteh

Rapid, broken, expressive. Letters merge and overlap. Dots vanish. The script of personal voice.

Individual Letters

The beauty of Persian calligraphy exists even at the level of a single character.

ا

Alef

The vertical stroke. Simplicity itself — and therefore the most demanding letter to write perfectly.

ع

Eyn

The most complex letter in the alphabet. Its forms change dramatically across the four positions.

ن

Nun

The bowl and tail. Nun's descending curve is one of Nastaliq's most characteristic gestures.

ه

He

Four entirely different forms depending on position. The chameleon of the Persian alphabet.
